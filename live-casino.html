Live roulette: European-first, with house edge in plain numbers

European roulette is the default recommendation for any 888bet live player who cares about house edge. A single-zero wheel produces a 2.7% edge against you on every bet. American double-zero wheels almost double that to 5.26%. French roulette with La Partage or En Prison rules drops the edge on even-money bets to 1.35%, which is the lowest available on a roulette table anywhere. Live blackjack: rules variants are everything

Live blackjack dealt by Evolution typically runs 6–8 decks with dealer stands soft 17 — expect 0.5–0.6% house edge with optimal basic strategy.
